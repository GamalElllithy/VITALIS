# Design principles

- Accessibility: keyboard, screen reader, contrast, and focus support. TODO: agree conformance targets.
- Readability: plain language, legible type, clear hierarchy.
- Consistent UI: reuse patterns and terminology.
- Clear health information: show units, time ranges, provenance, missingness, and limitations.
- Explainable signals: explain context and reason; do not present as a diagnosis.
- Responsive design: support expected devices and contexts. TODO: prioritize devices.
- Avoid misleading visualizations: honest scales, labeled axes, appropriate charts, visible gaps, and no unsupported certainty.
