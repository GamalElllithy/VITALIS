# Medical safety boundaries

- VITALIS is a prototype and is not a medical diagnostic system.
- Signals may indicate patterns or deviations in available data; they are not diagnoses.
- VITALIS does not diagnose or treat and does not replace medical professionals.
- Future recommendations must be evidence-based, reviewed, and presented with limitations.
- Simulated data must be clearly labeled and never represented as research evidence.
- Do not make unsupported clinical claims or imply unvalidated methods are safe or effective.
